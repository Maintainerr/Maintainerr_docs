Translations

The Maintainerr web UI can be shown in your own language. The translations come from the community, through Weblate.

Pick your language​

Open Settings -> General, choose a language under Display language, and press Save.

  • The choice is stored in your browser, not on the server. Everyone who opens Maintainerr picks their own language, and every browser and device is set separately.
  • Until you pick one, Maintainerr follows the language your browser asks for.
  • Anything not translated yet falls back to English.

English is the source language and is always complete. The other languages are still being worked on, and how much is done depends on how far their translators have got.

note

Only the web UI is translated. Notifications and emails are sent in English.

Help us translate Maintainerr​

Every language on the list needs help, and you do not have to sign up for much. Translate five strings today, and they go out with the next release to everyone who runs Maintainerr in your language.

It all happens on Weblate. Pick your language, sign in, and type the words in your browser. There is nothing to install, no git, and no pull request. Stop whenever you like, and the next person carries on where you left off.

  • Not sure how to word something? Leave a comment on that string in Weblate. Skipping it is fine too - a missing translation is shown in English, so nothing breaks.
  • If the English text has a placeholder such as {count}, keep it in your translation. Weblate warns you when one is missing, and the same check runs again before the translation is accepted.
  • Missing a language? Ask for it on Weblate and start it yourself. Once work has begun, it is added to the Display language list.
  • Weblate gathers the finished work and opens a pull request on the Maintainerr repository. A maintainer reads it, and your words ship with the next release.

Thank you. Every string helps someone use Maintainerr in the language they think in.
